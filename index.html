About Anime Sekai
🌸
Anime Sekai is a fan-made anime trivia platform built for anime lovers of all levels. Test your knowledge solo, challenge friends in real time, and climb the ranks from Casual Watcher to True Otaku.

Built with love for the anime community. 🎌
